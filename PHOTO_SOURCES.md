# SkillSetu – Photography Registry, Provenance & Attribution (PHOTO_SOURCES.md)

This document records the provenance, attribution, license, and verified subject matter for all photographs used across the **SkillSetu** platform (Track 4 – UnlockED: Localized Vocational Skill Verification).

---

## 1. Ethics & Quality Commitment

1. **Zero AI-Generated Faces or People**: No synthetic, deepfake, or generative-AI people are used anywhere on SkillSetu.
2. **Honest Attribution & Context**: Every photograph is documented with its verified source, creator, and license. We never fabricate state origins, names, or credentials.
3. **Dignity of Labour**: Workers are depicted engaged in real vocational craft with genuine tools (screwdrivers, wrenches, impact drivers, masonry mallets, concrete mixers, industrial sewing machines).
4. **No Unrelated Stock Clichés**: Previous placeholder imagery (e.g. bathroom faucets for plumbers, flower vases/towels for carpenters) has been replaced with authentic, hands-on trade craftsmanship.
5. **Open Commercial Licensing**: All imagery is sourced under verified open licenses (Pexels License and Unsplash License) that grant free commercial and non-commercial web use.

## 5. Local High-Resolution Asset Pipeline (About & Women in Trades)

To guarantee maximum visual sharpness without pixelation or cloud latency, the About section utilizes local multi-format assets:
- **Original Pristine File**: `frontend/public/images/textile-worker-original.jpg` (2048 × 1365, 344 KB).
- **WebP Optimized Asset**: `frontend/public/images/textile-worker.webp` (2048 × 1365, 483 KB) with subtle unsharp masking for enhanced tool and textile edge definition.
- **High-Res JPG Asset**: `frontend/public/images/textile-worker.jpg` (2048 × 1365, 609 KB).
- **HTML Implementation**: Implemented with `<picture>`, serving the high-efficiency WebP asset to modern browsers, falling back to local JPG, and falling back via `onError` to the remote 2048px CDN URL.
- **Ethics Verification**: 100% natural facial preservation; zero AI alteration, smoothing, or synthetic facial replacement.

---

## 6. Resilient Fallback Handling

Every `<img>` element in the SkillSetu frontend includes an `onError` fallback handler. If an image fails to load due to offline status or network drops, it gracefully reverts to the verified national infrastructure photograph (`hero_general`), preventing broken image indicators or layout shifts.
